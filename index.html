The framework

The P&L shows one cost. Good founders price all three.

Most advice stops at "consider opportunity cost." We measure it — plus the two costs no spreadsheet tracks.

01

Explicit cost

The cash out the door: invoices, salaries, software, ad spend. It's the only cost your accountant sees.

02

Implicit cost

What you give up to do it. Your hours, your team's focus, and the return of the next-best option you didn't choose.

03

Social cost

The trust a decision spends with your team, customers, investors, and community. Invisible today, expensive next quarter.

Where do the credit figures come from?

What's a "social cost," exactly?

The goodwill a decision spends — a layoff that shakes the remaining team, a price change that annoys loyal customers, a missed promise to an investor. It rarely shows up on a P&L, but it shows up in retention, referrals, and your next raise.
